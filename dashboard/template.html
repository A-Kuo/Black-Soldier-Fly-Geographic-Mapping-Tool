Read with care

Most BSF records come from iNaturalist, while the insect denominator also includes museum and survey datasets. A high rate can mean active iNaturalist users as much as many flies.

Within-country GDP gradients may partly reflect urbanisation. Richer regions are more urban, and BSF is associated with compost and organic waste, but urban observers may also photograph it more readily.
